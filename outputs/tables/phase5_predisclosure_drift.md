# Phase 5: pre-disclosure drift (in-sample, entry dates 2021-09-01 to 2024-02-29)

_What the disclosure lag costs a copier: the stock's return minus SPY's from the close on the TRANSACTION date (the senator's trade; the first trading day on or after it) to the close on the ENTRY date (the first trading day after the disclosure, when the copier acts). Every in-scope purchase and sale record with an in-sample entry date and prices on both dates counts once. t-statistics cluster by calendar month of entry. From `data/processed/event_panel.csv` (column gap_x)._

**Sign convention.** The drift is the stock's move, whatever the side. For a PURCHASE a positive drift means the price ran up before the copier could buy (a cost). For a SALE a negative drift means the price fell before the copier could sell (a cost). "Mean cost to the copier" flips the sign for sales so that positive always means the lag hurt the copier.
