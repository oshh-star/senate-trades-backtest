## Notes

- **Risk-matched SPY** = RF + k (SPY - RF) with k = sd(strategy monthly excess return) / sd(SPY monthly excess return): k = 1.445 for series A, 0.727 for series B (k > 1 means borrowing at RF). Built monthly (its monthly volatility matches the strategy's exactly); its drawdown uses the daily version with the same k.
- **Senate universe, equal weight**: each month, every symbol with an in-scope senator purchase whose entry date (first trading day after disclosure) is in the 12 months up to the previous month's last trading day, equally weighted at that close and held for the month. Symbols without prices are left out (Phase 5 section of outputs/data_quality.md). It separates WHICH stocks senators buy from WHEN they trade them.
- **Turnover** (series A and B only): each day, 0.5 x sum of |weight after trades - weight before trades (after drift)|; series B's weights include its cash; summed within each month, annualised as mean x 12. **Break-even one-way cost** = (mean monthly return of the strategy minus its risk-matched SPY) x 12 / (2 x annual turnover), in basis points; when that excess return is not positive, there is no break-even cost.
- **Minimum detectable alpha** = 2 x OLS standard error of the monthly alpha x 12: the annualised alpha that would have produced t = 2 with this residual volatility and number of months.
- **Newey-West**: Bartlett kernel, 3 lags (statsmodels HAC).
- **Sterling check** (series A for a UK investor, GBPUSD=X): series A 19.70% a year in GBP (volatility 18.39%), against 22.95% in USD; SPY 16.17% in GBP (volatility 13.06%) against 19.32% in USD.
- **Warm-up (L2)**: the book was built from disclosures from 2020-09-01; returns are measured from 2021-09-01. The Phase 4 empty start is variant V17 (in the variants table).
