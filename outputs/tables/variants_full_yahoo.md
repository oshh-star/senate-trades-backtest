**Prices: Yahoo, the final headline source (R3, S2).** Stocks in the survivorship ledger (mostly delisted names Yahoo no longer serves) have no prices: their events drop out here and their purchases are skipped in the book; V15 (SPY fill) bounds the effect.

_Built by `python run_variants.py` (src/variants.py). Each pre-registered variant is run ALONE against the headline: one config switch, everything else as the headline (docs/DECISIONS.md, "Pre-registered variants"). Series A = return on capital deployed (headline); B = fixed pot. Monthly statistics as in outputs/tables/phase5_insample_results.md: annualised geometric return, volatility, Sharpe on excess returns, CAPM and Carhart alphas (annualised, OLS t), maximum drawdown of the daily index. "Purchases copied" = purchases the book bought inside the window. Δ = variant minus headline. V11 (2023-09 to 2026-08) and V12 (2016-09 to 2026-08) cover different dates, so their Δ compare different periods. Unrounded numbers: the .csv._

**EXPLORATORY.** Not one of the registered confirmatory tests (H0-H4, docs/DECISIONS.md, "Out-of-sample test plan"; outputs/tables/confirmatory_oos.md). Roughly 150 cells were examined in-sample, so a few cells in a table like this one will reach |t| = 2 by chance.**
